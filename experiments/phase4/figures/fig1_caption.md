Fig. 1. Magnitude (top) and delay (bottom; phase lag / 2πf) of the PX4 v1.16 multicopter gyro filter stack, computed with the loop model's own filter functions at its filter rate (6400 Hz). (a) PX4 default (D-09): gyro low-pass 40 Hz (2nd order) and D-term low-pass 30 Hz (PT1); the ESC-RPM notch bank is off. (b) The same stack with the ESC-RPM notch bank on at its defaults (bandwidth 15 Hz, 3 harmonics) for 4 motors at 84.8 Hz, the fit-half median hover frequency. Shaded: the crossover band f_c = 4-15 Hz (D-28) and the noise band from 5 f_c to 780 Hz (hatched from 20 to 75 Hz, where its start depends on f_c). The spikes in the delay curves of (b) sit at the notch centres (84.8, 169.5 and 254.3 Hz): the phase of each notch swings from lag to lead where its magnitude is almost zero. They lie far above the crossover band, so they do not affect the stability margins at crossover. The bank does lower the margins a little: the tails of its 12 notches add 1.8 ms of delay throughout the crossover band (2.6° of phase lag at 4 Hz, 10.0° at 15 Hz). Whenever the bank is on, the margins in this work include this lag, because the figure and the loop model use the same code.
