## 3. A4 question 2: can the identification band be extended?

**How the two levers work:**
- **Longer Welch segments do not raise the band's upper edge.** They give finer frequency resolution. In exchange they halve the number of averages, so coherence estimates get noisier, and they drop every piece shorter than one segment.
- **A lower coherence gate admits noisier points.**
- **Both change the D-14 gate itself:** the "6 coherent frequencies" rule was set at 0.78 Hz resolution and coherence 0.5.

I ran the identification for **all 28 included cases**, not only the five, so that the comparison does not select on the outcome:

| V1 band to 40 Hz | 27 | 22 | 18 | 1 | 1 | 0 |
| V2 band to 40 Hz, coherence 0.3 | 23 | 18 | 15 | 2 | 5 | 0 |

**How many of the five would pass:**
- 1-3, depending on the variant: 94bd7879 from V1, b27d09e3 from V2, 852b8368 only with 2.56 s segments.
- **4e73d30d and b79d2e3d never pass.** Their peaks were already measured at high coherence, so the band was never their problem.

**Why this is not a fix:**
- **Every variant loses other identifications:** mostly the slow drones (225bab55, 5cecf734, 7e8f0064, df9a5d9e; in V4 also 94bd7879 roll and fcdef3a8 roll) fail the fit-error gates over the wider band. **V3 turns a passing drone into a nominal failure** (9ca62be3 roll). **Net passes go down (18 → 13-18).**

**Recommendation:** keep the D-14 settings. Choosing the identification settings after seeing which drones fail would tune the method on its own outcome.

## 4. A4 question 3: why $M_s$ ≤ 3 for the worst case?

**It is a convention, not a sourced value.** No textbook gives a worst-case $M_s$ over a parameter box. What the sources do say (verified in the texts):
- **Skogestad and Postlethwaite (2005), Multivariable Feedback Control, 2nd ed., Sec. 2.4.3, p. 36:** "Typically, it is required that $M_S$ is less than about 2 (6 dB) … A large value of $M_S$ or $M_T$ (larger than about 4) indicates poor performance as well as poor robustness."
- **Åström and Murray, Feedback Systems (2006 draft, Sec. 12.2):** the shortest distance from the Nyquist curve to −1 is $1/M_s$; "the maximum sensitivity $M_s$ is thus a good robustness measure".

**So $M_{s,wc}$ = 3 sits midway** between the usual requirement (about 2) and the level called poor (about 4). Every plant of the set then stays at least 1/3 away from −1 (PM ≥ 19.2°, GM ≥ 3.5 dB). At 4, the worst case would be allowed to reach the level the textbook calls poor robustness.

| $M_{s,wc}$ | Implied PM / GM | Real, ±20 % +1 (of 23 nominal passes) | Real, ±15 % +1 | Synthetic, ±20 % +1 (of 498) | Synthetic, ±15 % +1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2 | 29.0° / 6.02 dB | 9 (39 %) | 10 (43 %) | 203 (40.8 %) | 244 (49.0 %) |
| 2.5 | 23.1° / 4.44 dB | 14 (61 %) | 17 (74 %) | 419 (84.1 %) | 456 (91.6 %) |
| **3** | 19.2° / 3.52 dB | **18 (78 %)** | 22 (96 %) | 483 (97.0 %) | 495 (99.4 %) |
| 3.5 | 16.4° / 2.92 dB | 22 (96 %) | 23 (100 %) | 497 (99.8 %) | 498 (100.0 %) |
| 4 | 14.4° / 2.50 dB | 23 (100 %) | 23 (100 %) | 497 (99.8 %) | 498 (100.0 %) |

At 4, the worst-case check no longer rejects any real flown setting.

## 5. A4 question 4: is 22 % "reference unsafe" acceptable?

**Recommendation: report it as a finding; keep the flagged drones in the real test set; do not exclude them.** The data:
1. **Every flagged flown setting can be made safe by the filters alone.** This holds for all 10: the 5 here and the 5 nominal failures.
   - 91-367 of 432 settings on a grid over f_gyro 15-300 Hz × f_dterm 10-200 Hz pass the separate limits.
   - The best setting is always the highest cutoffs (300 / 200 Hz).
   - So the optimizer has a safe label for each of them. They are exactly the drones on which a safety-aware tuner shows its value.
   - **Caveat: stability only.** Raising the cutoffs costs noise. Mode A (not noisier than the reference) may therefore be infeasible for them, while Mode B is feasible. Task 3 will quantify this.
2. **The THESIS already defines the handling:** 7.8 step 4.1 returns the reference with the flag "reference unsafe" and counts these cases; D-15 says to report "reference outside limits".
3. **Excluding them would select the test set on the check's own outcome.** It would hide the drones that exercise the repair and fallback paths, and it would hide that the check is conservative: these drones fly well.
4. **The flag rate is a dial, not a fixed fact:**
   - 22 % (5 of 23) at $M_{s,wc}$ 3 with ±20 % / +1 sample;
   - 4 % at $M_{s,wc}$ 3.5, or with ±15 %;
   - 0 % at $M_{s,wc}$ 4, or with a half-sample delay step.
   The paper should report the flag share together with the limits.
