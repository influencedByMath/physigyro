## 1. What every number means

- **Reference:**
  - synthetic drones and real-noise cases: the PX4 v1.16 default (D-09);
  - real-gain cases: the pilot's flown setting (D-15). The log's own default is reported as well.
- **Optimum:** optimized under the D-37 limits:
  - nominal PM ≥ 30°, $M_s$ ≤ 2.0, GM ≥ 6 dB;
  - every corner plant of ±20 % on $b$ and $\tau_m$, plus 1 controller sample: Nyquist-stable, $M_s$ ≤ 3.0;
  - the coarse search runs on the fast grid; the refinement and every reported number use the full grid.
- **Mode A:** the best PM with noise no higher than the reference ($\tilde N \le 1$).
  - ΔPM = PM(optimum) − PM(reference).
  - Δτ_F and Δτ_D are the delay changes of the gyro path and the D-term path at crossover.
- **Mode B:** the least relative noise $\tilde N$ with PM ≥ PM(reference).

**Reading it:**
- **Synthetic and real noise agree.** At equal noise the optimum gains about 3° of PM (median), mostly by cutting the D-term path delay (about −2.5 ms). At equal PM, Mode B removes 38-46 % of the motor-command noise ($\tilde N$ 0.54-0.62).
- **The gain grows with the crossover:** synthetic median ΔPM 2.1° at 3-4 Hz, 3.4° at 4-5 Hz, 4.7° at 5-6.5 Hz, 6.4° at 6.5-8 Hz and 9.2° at 8-10 Hz (n = 17).

- Mode B $\tilde N$ above 1 means more noise than the flown setting. This is the price of making an unsafe flown setting safe.

## 8. Preliminary A5 recommendation (decided 2026-10-19 as D-38: 10,000 cases, as recommended; plan `notes/task4_plan.md`)

**Measured cost** of the D-37 optimizer (Modes A and B; the same machinery Task 4 labels need):
- on one worker: about 16-17 s per two-peak Scenario A case (57 notch options) and about 10 s per Scenario B case;
- on 6 workers: **about 3.4 s per case** of wall time.

**Recommendation:**
- **10,000 cases with exactly the D-37 optimizer**, so the labels are safe by construction and consistent with RQ1.
- Scenario B at its natural share (about 10 %, 5 generator sources).
- **5,000 as the fallback** if the schedule tightens.

**Cheaper labels, not recommended without a new check and your decision:**
- Fast-grid-only labels break the limits on the full grid (1 of 4 planning cases).
- A greedy notch search (NF0 first, then NF1: 15 instead of 57 options) would be about 3× faster, but it changes the label algorithm and needs its own brute-force check.
- The real-noise cases with **test** spectra must never enter training.

## 9. Rulings I made during Task 3 (each with its cost if wrong)

1. **Synthetic gains are designed on the plant including the D-33 chip lower bound** (the planning run designed without it).
   - Why: this follows D-33 and Tier 3 step 2a.
   - Cost: the kept share differs slightly from the 85 % estimate (now 86.8 %).
2. **Real-gain cases need identification and windows from the same log**, and the test identification uses `test_logs.json` only, as the fit half did.
   - Cost: a test vehicle whose windows come from a fallback log is not counted.
3. **Spectra:**
   - real-gain: the power mean of the case's own windows on its own axis;
   - real-noise: the vehicle spectrum (D-24 (2)) with the vehicle's chip for the D-33 delay.
4. **Real Scenario B notch positions use the window-mean RPM** (consistent with the window spectra).
   - Cost: b27d09e3 roll's worst-corner $M_s$ is 3.04 instead of 3.03.
5. **Scenario A notch centres are the two most prominent peaks** above the robust floor (the Phase 3 rule), not the two highest.
6. **Mode C only for the 4 Fig. 4 drones.** Table 2 needs Modes A and B only.
7. **Lazy worst-case check:** coarse candidates in objective order must pass the nominal and worst-case limits and the noise or PM constraint on the full grid before refinement starts.
8. **The 4 test cases below 3 Hz stay in every result** (question in Section 4).
9. **D-13 tracking-notch spectra are also used for the real-noise Scenario B vehicles** (5 fit, 3 test), checked against the library spectra.
10. **Table 2 gets a column "ΔPM, safe references only"**, because for flagged references the Mode A gain is a repair.
